    Our Story

    Oud began with a simple idea: that a cup of coffee tastes better when there is music in the room. Our space is built around a giant oud and a golden dallah, the symbols of Arab hospitality and song.

    We roast with care, brew with cardamom the traditional way, and play the classic maqamat all day long. Come for the coffee, stay for the melody.
